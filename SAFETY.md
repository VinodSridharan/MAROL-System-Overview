## Medical Content Policy

### What We DON'T Do
❌ Provide external medical advice  
❌ Diagnose conditions  
❌ Recommend treatments  
❌ Interpret clinical findings  
❌ Answer "What should I do about..." questions  

### What We DO
✅ Answer factual questions about YOUR uploaded medical documents  
✅ Extract information from YOUR lab results, reports, imaging findings  
✅ Search YOUR medical records for specific information  
✅ Summarize YOUR uploaded clinical notes  

### Examples

**ALLOWED** (Factual extraction from user's corpus):
- "What does my MRI report say about the findings?"
- "What medications are listed in my discharge summary?"
- "What was my cholesterol level in the 2025 lab results?"
- "Summarize the treatment plan from my doctor's notes"

**REJECTED** (Medical advice/interpretation):
- "Should I be worried about these lab results?"
- "What does this diagnosis mean for me?"
- "Is this medication safe?"
- "Do I need to see a specialist?"

### Important Disclaimers
1. **Not Medical Advice**: MAROL extracts information from YOUR documents but does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.
2. **Consult Your Doctor**: Always consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical decisions.
3. **No Liability**: MAROL is not responsible for medical decisions based on extracted information.
4. **Your Responsibility**: You are responsible for verifying medical information with your healthcare provider.

### HIPAA Considerations (COLLABORATOR Mode)
- Medical documents may contain Protected Health Information (PHI)
- User is responsible for HIPAA compliance when uploading PHI
- Collaborator keys for medical use cases: contact us for HIPAA-compliant workspace setup
- Data retention: Medical corpora deleted per agreed schedule
- Encryption: All data encrypted at rest and in transit
