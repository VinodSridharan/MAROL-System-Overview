# MAROL Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective Date:** January 20, 2026

This policy defines acceptable use of MAROL's Demo and Evaluation tiers.

---

## ✅ Permitted Uses

- **Portfolio evaluation** for recruiting and technical interviews
- **Proof-of-concept testing** with your own documents (that you own or have permission to use)
- **Architectural review** and technical demonstrations
- **Learning and experimentation** with RAG/multi-agent systems

---

## ❌ Prohibited Uses

### Abuse & Exploitation
- **Automated scraping** or bulk querying to extract system prompts or proprietary algorithms
- **Rate limit circumvention** (creating multiple accounts, rotating IPs)
- **Malicious uploads** (malware, scripts designed to exploit vulnerabilities)
- **Credential sharing** (one Evaluation key = one user; sharing violates terms)

### Content Violations
- Uploading content you don't own or have permission to use (pirated books, confidential employer documents under NDA)
- Uploading illegal content (CSAM, extremist material, instructions for illegal activities)
- Using MAROL to generate harmful content (financial scams, medical misinformation, hate speech)

### Competitive Intelligence
- **Reverse engineering** prompts or algorithms via prompt injection
- **Benchmarking for competitive purposes** without explicit permission (contact us if you're building a competing product and want to cite MAROL)

---

## 🚨 Consequences of Violation

**First offense:** Warning + temporary suspension (24 hours)  
**Repeat offenses:** Permanent key revocation + IP block  
**Severe violations** (illegal content, security exploits): Immediate permanent ban + law enforcement notification if warranted

---

## 📊 Fair Use Guidelines

**Demo tier:** 5 queries/min, 50/day is generous for evaluation. If you need more, request an Evaluation key.

**Evaluation tier:** No hard rate limits, but egregious abuse (e.g., 10,000 queries/day) may trigger review.

**"Fair use" definition:** Normal human usage for intended evaluation purposes. If you're unsure whether your use case is acceptable, ask first.

---

## 🔄 Policy Updates

This policy may be updated to address new abuse patterns. Continued use after updates = acceptance.
