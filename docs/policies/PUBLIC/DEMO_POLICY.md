# Demo Tier Policy

**Effective Date:** January 28, 2026

## Purpose

Try MAROL in 2 minutes: upload 5 file types (text, PDF, Word, YouTube, audio), ask 5 questions, see multi-agent RAG with evidence badges. No signup required.

## What You Get

- **1 text file** (TXT) with balloon questions
- **1 PDF** with balloon questions  
- **1 Word doc** (DOCX) with balloon questions
- **1 YouTube video** (auto-transcribed) with summary + 5 balloon questions
- **1 audio file** (MP3/WAV, auto-transcribed) with summary + 5 balloon questions
- **5 questions total** across all ingested content
- **Voice/mic input** (unlimited, asks questions about your ingested content)

## Session Duration

- **24 hours** from first upload
- After 24h, **all content automatically deleted** (no human review, no recovery)

## Data Deletion (Automatic)

Your demo session is deleted automatically by the system after 24 hours. This process:
- **Runs every hour** (automated cleanup job)
- **No human involvement** (deletion is immediate and final)
- **No export available** for Demo tier (upgrade to Evaluation for export)

## Upgrade Path

- **Evaluation key (7 days, free):** 10 files per type, unlimited questions, export with citations
- **Contact us** for ongoing projects or production use

## Your Data

- **Retention:** 24 hours, then automatic permanent deletion
- **Isolation:** Your demo session is private (other users cannot access)
- **No recovery:** Once deleted, data cannot be recovered

**By uploading, you agree to 24-hour retention and automatic deletion.**
